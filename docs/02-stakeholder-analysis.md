# 02 - Stakeholder Analysis

## Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder        | Role                    | Interest                      | Risk/Concern                    |
| ------------------ | ----------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| CEO                | Business decision maker | Growth and profitability      | Financial and reputational risk |
| IT Manager         | System owner            | System stability and security | Technical vulnerabilities       |
| Compliance Officer | Regulatory compliance   | GDPR and policy adherence     | Legal and compliance risks      |
| Customers          | End users               | Data security and usability   | Data privacy concerns           |

## Stakeholder Insights

* The CEO is focused on scaling the business but lacks visibility into risks.
* The IT Manager handles operations but without structured governance.
* The Compliance Officer has limited tools to enforce policies.
* Customers expect secure and reliable services.

## Conclusion

There is a clear misalignment between business growth and risk management, requiring structured governance.
