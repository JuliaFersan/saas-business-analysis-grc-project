# 05 - Risk Assessment

## Risk Matrix

| Risk                  | Impact | Likelihood | Risk Level |
| --------------------- | ------ | ---------- | ---------- |
| Data breach           | High   | Medium     | High       |
| Unauthorized access   | High   | High       | Critical   |
| GDPR non-compliance   | High   | Medium     | High       |
| Manual process errors | Medium | High       | High       |

## Risk Analysis

* Unauthorized access is the most critical risk due to lack of controls.
* Manual processes significantly increase operational risk.
* Compliance gaps expose the company to legal consequences.

## Conclusion

The organization lacks basic risk management controls and requires immediate action.
