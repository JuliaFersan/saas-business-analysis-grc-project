# 08 - Recommendations

## Strategic Recommendations

* Implement Identity and Access Management (IAM) system
* Automate onboarding workflows
* Define governance and risk management framework
* Adopt ISO 27001 controls

## Operational Improvements

* Reduce manual intervention
* Improve process efficiency
* Standardize procedures

## Security Enhancements

* Introduce RBAC and MFA
* Enable continuous monitoring
* Conduct regular risk assessments

## Business Impact

* Increased efficiency
* Reduced risks
* Improved compliance
* Better customer trust

## Conclusion

These recommendations provide a roadmap for sustainable growth and risk management.
